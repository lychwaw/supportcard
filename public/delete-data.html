Delete your data

There are two ways to remove your information from SupportCard. You can clear your records and keep your account, or you can delete the account and everything in it.

Delete your account and all of your data

This removes your account and everything attached to it. It cannot be undone.

  1. Open SupportCard and sign in.
  2. Tap the cog on the home screen to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when asked. You are signed out straight away.

What is deleted: your sign-in details and email address, your name and profile, your children's profiles, calendar events, expense requests and receipts, messages, uploaded documents, care check-ins and any saved locations, your goals, contacts and reports, and any professional access you had granted.

What is kept, and for how long: deletion is permanent and all of the above is removed from our systems within 30 days. Records we are required by law to keep, such as proof of a payment already made, are kept only for as long as the law requires and are not used for anything else. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle, within 30 days.

One thing worth knowing: SupportCard is shared between two co-parents. Records your co-parent created stay on their own account, because they are their records. Deleting your account does not delete theirs.

Delete your records but keep your account

If you want to clear your history without closing the account:

  1. Open Settings from the home screen.
  2. Tap Wipe family history.
  3. Confirm when asked.

What is deleted: all of your expense requests, calendar events, care check-ins and saved locations. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

What is kept: your account, your children's profiles and your co-parent link, so you can carry on using the app with a clean record.

If you cannot open the app

Email info@southsphere.global from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm your identity, action the request and reply to confirm, within 30 days.
